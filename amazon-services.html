Frequently asked questions

How quickly can you take over an existing Amazon account?

Most handovers take one to two weeks. We start with an account audit — listings, ad structure, account health flags — before changing anything, so nothing breaks mid-handover.

Do you work with brand-registered sellers only, or also unregistered accounts?

Both. Brand Registry unlocks A+ Content, Storefronts and better IP protection, so if you're not enrolled yet we'll usually recommend it, but it isn't a requirement to start.

Can you manage Amazon and our own website at the same time?

Yes — a lot of our accounts run Amazon alongside Shopify, Magento or WordPress stores, with pricing and stock kept in sync across both rather than treated as separate jobs.

How is pricing structured — management fee, ad spend percentage, or both?

It depends on account size and scope. Smaller accounts are usually a flat monthly fee; larger multi-marketplace accounts often combine a base fee with a percentage of ad spend. We'll quote this after the audit.

What happens if our account gets a policy warning or suspension?

Account health and case handling are part of the core service, not an add-on. We respond to warnings and work the case directly with Seller Support to get it resolved.
