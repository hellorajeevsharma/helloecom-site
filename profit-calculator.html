Frequently asked questions

How does the marketplace profit calculator work?

Enter your selling price, product cost and other costs once. The calculator applies each marketplace's commission, fulfilment fee, VAT or GST and ad spend, and shows profit per unit, margin, break-even ad spend and monthly profit side by side.

Are the marketplace fees in the calculator accurate?

The fee values are starting examples, not official rates. Commission and fulfilment fees depend on your category, size, weight and marketplace, and they change over time. Open "Tweak this marketplace" and enter the rates from your own seller account.

What is break-even ad spend?

It is the highest share of sales you can spend on advertising before the unit stops making a profit. If your actual ad spend is above it, that unit loses money.

Does the calculator save or send my numbers?

No. Everything runs in your browser. Nothing you type is saved or sent to HelloEcom. Use the Copy summary button if you want to keep the result.

Can I add a marketplace that is not listed?

Yes. Type its name under "Add your own marketplace", press Add, then use "Tweak this marketplace" to enter its fees.
