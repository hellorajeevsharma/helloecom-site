Advanced Magento SEO

Magento gives you deep control over URLs, categories and layered navigation. That flexibility is also why large catalogues pick up crawl waste, duplicate pages and slow templates that need specialist attention.

Our Magento SEO work is built around large, multi-filter catalogues and multi-store setups, where small configuration choices have big effects on what search engines crawl and index.

  • Layered navigation and filter URL control, so crawl budget goes to pages that earn traffic
  • Canonical, noindex and robots rules set correctly across categories, products and store views
  • URL rewrites, redirects and sitemap settings audited and cleaned up
  • Performance work: full page cache, Varnish or Redis, image and script optimisation

Frequently asked questions

Can you take over a Magento store someone else built?

Yes. We start with a technical and content audit of the existing build before making changes, so we understand what's custom, what's stock, and what's fragile.

Do you support both Magento Open Source and Adobe Commerce?

Yes, we build and maintain both, including the extra features (B2B, content staging) that come with Adobe Commerce.

Who handles server and hosting — you or us?

Either. We coordinate directly with your hosting provider or sysadmin on server-related tasks, or we can recommend hosting if you don't have it set up yet.

How do you handle Magento version upgrades and security patches?

As part of ongoing admin and maintenance — patches are applied on a tested staging copy first, then pushed live, rather than patched directly on the live store.

Can Magento product data stay in sync with our Amazon and other marketplace listings?

Yes — this is a common setup for our clients: Magento as the master catalogue, with feeds kept consistent across Amazon and other marketplaces we manage.

What makes Magento SEO different from other platforms?

Magento stores often have large catalogues, layered navigation filters and multiple store views, which can create duplicate or low-value URLs. Magento SEO focuses on controlling what gets crawled and indexed, plus speed and catalogue-level page optimisation.

Can you manage our product listings in the Magento admin?

Yes. We create and update listings, run bulk updates and imports, manage categories, attributes, images and CMS content, and keep caches and indexes refreshed, so your catalogue stays accurate without taking up your developers' time.
