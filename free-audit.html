Frequently asked questions

Is the audit really free?

Yes. There is no charge and no obligation to hire us afterwards.

It is a quick review of one area, either Amazon Ads or SEO, based on what you share with us. You get the top findings and what to fix first. It is not a full, deep-dive audit.

Do you need my passwords or login details?

No. Please never send passwords. We work from your public store or website link, plus screenshots, exports or read-only access that you control.

How long does it take?

We reply within one business day to confirm what we need. Once we have it, we aim to send your findings within 5 working days.

Will you try to sell me services afterwards?

We will tell you what we would fix first, and you can ask us to help with it. There is no pressure and no obligation.

Which marketplaces and platforms do you cover?

Our Amazon experience is mainly the UK, Europe and India. For SEO we work with Shopify, Magento, WordPress and custom sites. If yours is different, mention it in the form and we will tell you if we can help.

What happens to the information I share?

We use it only to prepare your audit and to contact you about it. See our Privacy Policy for details.
