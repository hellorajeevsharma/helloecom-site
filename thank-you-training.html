Get a head start while you wait

A few small things you can do today. They make your first session much more useful.

Learn SEO

  • Verify your website in Google Search Console (it is free).
  • List the 10 pages that matter most for your revenue.
  • Search your main product keyword and note who ranks, and what type of page they use.

Learn Amazon Advertising

  • Work out your profit per unit after Amazon fees and product cost.
  • Break-even ACoS is your profit margin before ad spend, as a % of the selling price. Know yours.
  • If you have an account, export your last 60 days of search-term report to bring to the first session.

Learn Google Ads

  • Create a free GA4 property and a Google Ads account (no need to spend anything).
  • Write down 3 actions that count as a "win" for your business, such as a purchase or an enquiry.
  • Try Keyword Planner with your top 5 product or service terms.
