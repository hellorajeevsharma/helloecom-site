What you will be able to do

  • Explain how the Amazon ad auction and ranking work
  • Build Sponsored Products, Brands and Display campaigns with a clean structure
  • Do keyword and product-targeting research, and use match types well

Frequently asked questions

Do I need an Amazon seller account to join?

It helps but is not essential. If you have an account you can practise on it. If not, we work with practice data and worked examples so you can still learn the full process.

Is this course only for people selling on Amazon.com?

No. The principles apply across Amazon marketplaces, including the UK and EU. We point out where features and rules differ by marketplace.

Will I learn ACoS, TACoS and break-even maths?

Yes. A full module covers how to calculate break-even ACoS from your margins and how to use ACoS, TACoS and ROAS to make bid and budget decisions.

Does it cover Sponsored Brands and Sponsored Display, or only Sponsored Products?

All three. Sponsored Products gets the most time because it is where most sellers start, then Brands and Display are covered with when and why to use them.

Is the course live? Can I ask questions?

It is taught live, online or in person depending on location. You can ask questions during sessions and while you work on your capstone.

What does it cost and how long does it run?

Fees and duration depend on the format (group or one-to-one) and your level. Send the enquiry form and we will reply within one business day with the details.

Can my team join together?

Yes. We can run a session for a team and build it around your own catalogue and accounts. Mention team size and goals in your enquiry.
