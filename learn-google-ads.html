Curriculum

Modules build on each other. Each one ends with a practical task, and the capstone pulls it all together.

Module 1

How Google Ads works

The auction, Ad Rank, Quality Score, campaign types and how Google bills for clicks.

Module 2

Account structure & setup

Accounts, campaigns, ad groups, budgets, locations, languages and naming that scales.

Module 3

Search campaigns

Keyword research, match types, negatives, responsive search ads, extensions and landing pages.

Module 4

Shopping & Merchant Center

Product feeds, feed rules, product groups, Shopping campaign structure and common feed errors.

Module 5

Performance Max

Display & YouTube

Module 7

Conversion tracking

Module 8

Bidding, budgets & optimisation

Manual vs smart bidding, targets, budget pacing, testing, and a weekly optimisation routine.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need a Google Ads account or budget to learn?

You can start without spending. We teach setup and structure first, and can use practice data and worked examples. If you have an account, you can practise on it.

Will I learn Performance Max and Shopping as well as Search?

Yes. Search, Shopping and Performance Max each have their own module, along with Display and YouTube basics.

Does the course cover conversion tracking, GA4 and GTM?

Yes. A full module shows how to set up and test conversion tracking with GA4 and Google Tag Manager, including consent basics, so decisions rest on reliable data.

Is this suitable for beginners?

Yes. It starts from how the auction works and builds up. Anyone already running campaigns can join at a later module.

Is the course live? Can I ask questions?

It is taught live, online or in person depending on location. You can ask questions during sessions and while you work on your capstone.

What does it cost and how long does it run?

Fees and duration depend on the format (group or one-to-one) and your level. Send the enquiry form and we will reply within one business day with the details.

Can you train my team on our own account?

Yes. We can build sessions around your own account and data. Mention team size and goals in your enquiry.
